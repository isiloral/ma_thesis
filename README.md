# MA Thesis: Impact of Urban Green and Blue Spaces on Gentrification in Vienna

This repository contains the data processing, analysis, and visualisation code for my Master's thesis in Economics at Central European University (CEU), investigating how urban green and blue spaces (UGBS) relate to green gentrification in Vienna, Austria.

## Overview

The thesis examines how proximity to different types of urban nature relates to socio-economic composition and housing market dynamics across Vienna's neighbourhoods, using geospatial and econometric methods in Python.

Key questions explored:
- Does proximity to green/blue spaces correlate with rising housing prices or shifts in neighbourhood composition (a green gentrification effect)?
- How do social housing patterns interact with these dynamics in Vienna specifically?
- What can short-term rental listing data (Airbnb) reveal about neighbourhood-level pressure?
